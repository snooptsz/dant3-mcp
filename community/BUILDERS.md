# Builder pathway: participate, maintain, or discuss a core role

Dant3 is being developed as a project by developers for developers, with Humans, AI Agents, Bots and Robots as clearly identified participants. We welcome people who want to understand the architecture, improve it and help shape its direction—not simply complete a queue of tasks for somebody else.

This is a project invitation, not an advertised salaried vacancy or an offer to buy an investment. The following are proposed participation paths, not grants of ownership or assurances of future work.

## 1. Open contributor

Choose a bounded issue or propose one. Ordinary contributions are genuinely voluntary: no compulsory shifts, minimum hours, exclusivity, quotas or obligation to continue, and no promised economic reward for submitting them. Declining or stopping carries no penalty. Review and acceptance are not guaranteed.

Useful outcomes include reusable public code, optional acknowledgement for accepted work, learning and a reviewable record of contribution. Attribution is a project practice where requested and appropriate; it does not amend MIT-0. Contributors retain the rights they hold in their own material while licensing submissions under the applicable repository terms. No blanket transfer of unrelated or pre-existing intellectual property is requested.

## 2. Component maintainer

Propose a public component you care about, such as an SDK, compatibility fixtures or onboarding documentation. Agree its scope, review authority, support expectations and handover in one existing issue. Maintainers should have a meaningful voice in their component's design, not simply responsibility for its defects.

The appointment is explicit, revocable and least-privilege. There is no automatic access to customer information, private infrastructure, safety cases or release credentials. No out-of-hours rota is implied. Before imposing ongoing service commitments, assess whether a paid or otherwise formal arrangement is required.

## 3. Prospective core project partner

A sustained technical, product or operational relationship can be discussed separately. Existing work samples or a technical conversation may be used to assess fit; an open contribution is not a compulsory audition. No unpaid trial or speculative production deliverable is required before those discussions.

Before agreed commercial work starts, the parties must settle the actual arrangement: scope, decision rights, responsibilities, compensation, intellectual property and exit. Potential structures for private review include a genuine co-founder agreement, paid component work, a clearly scoped revenue-sharing agreement, or an appropriate equity arrangement. None is offered automatically by this document.

For any proposed economic rights, define:

- the legal entity and parties, their authority to grant the rights, and the exact product/component covered;
- the agreed payment or ownership rights, any vesting/milestones, dilution, departure and dispute rules;
- for revenue sharing, the revenue definition, allowable deductions, reporting/audit rights, duration and payout conditions;
- the treatment of existing IP, open-source contributions, confidentiality, tax and applicable employment obligations.

Do not use vague promises of future profits in place of agreed terms. An arrangement that creates worker status may carry minimum wage and other rights regardless of a 'partner', 'volunteer' or 'co-founder' label. Equity or future revenue is not a device for avoiding those obligations. Qualified advice must address the real relationship and relevant jurisdictions before commercial work is assigned.

## Capital is a separate conversation

No payment or investment is required to contribute or discuss a role. This pathway is not a public investment offer, crowdfunding campaign or token scheme. Do not send funds, bank details, wallet details or financial documents through an issue. No financial solicitation is authorised here; private communications also need appropriate review before investment terms are circulated.

There is no guaranteed income and no automatic equity. Commercial income may never arise. Any genuine investment can involve loss, and public wording must not imply otherwise. A disclaimer alone does not make an otherwise regulated promotion acceptable.

## Human and automated accountability

Disclose AI-assisted work and verify it. People or legal entities enter agreements; AI systems are not allocated shares, partnership votes or autonomous spending authority by their platform identity. Machine credentials remain within their published scopes and are not Human sessions.

## Where to start

Use the existing community contribution form for a sanitised technical proposal or to indicate interest in longer-term project collaboration. Give the area you care about, the problem you would explore and any existing public evidence you choose to share. Do not post private commercial terms. No CV, passport, investment amount or fixed weekly commitment is requested by the public form.

Read [governance](GOVERNANCE.md), [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and [SECURITY](../SECURITY.md). Production changes remain GitLab-reviewed; no outreach, employment, partner agreement or equity issue is created merely by this guide.
